# AdWorks workspace extension

Mode: Operate. Audience: Dubai advertising, printing, gifting and signage businesses. Extend the incumbent CRM world and the explicitly requested connected job workflow. The brief authorizes direct implementation with fictional AED records and browser-local storage.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Keep every advertising job traceable from enquiry and itemized quotation through artwork approval, in-house or outsourced production, delivery, billing and customer profit.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Inter, navy navigation, cool neutral canvas, white working surfaces, thin borders, compact controls and tabular AED figures. Restrained orange identifies AdWorks; status colors remain semantic and text-labelled.

STORY: A presenter opens a customer dossier, follows a mixed production job through its gates, records a partial delivery and compares earned revenue, itemized actual costs, profit and cash. A supplier payment settles a liability without creating a second expense.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Readable operational figures lead into a record-derived revenue-versus-cost chart, active job queue and the next approval or production action. Create enquiry sits beside the heading. Phone navigation fixes Home, Jobs, Customers, Finance and More to the bottom; More reaches every other working module.

FORM: Carry the inherited operational cockpit and customer dossier, using the established code-led direction and seed 37a017eb. The requested workflow and phone navigation settle composition; no new visual identity or concept selection is introduced.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Financial truth

Use integer cents. Accepted quotes book sales; immutable fulfillment quantities and prices earn revenue. Actual costs include consumed materials, entered labor/production/delivery and QC-accepted outsourced receipts. Purchase-order commitments and supplier payments are separate ledgers and never create additional cost. Customer receipts settle invoices; outstanding is invoiced less received, and unbilled is earned less invoiced. Costs incurred before fulfillment can make an unfinished job's earned profit negative. Show planned margin separately.

The small worked example is AED 200 fulfilled sales less AED 80 actual cost, giving AED 120 profit. This is a simulated operational view, without tax calculation or an accounting/audit claim. External messages, approvals, receipts and payments are clearly simulated. No backend, real transactions or credential changes.
