# Five-lead and project extension

The Blastline demo adds five independent, initially unconverted opportunities: two site projects, equipment sales, a dated physical rental and a customer-owned repair. The existing sales, rental, workshop and customer-ledger routes remain available. Data and external decisions/payments are fictional and browser-local.

Each lead requires an editable saved quotation, simulated sending and approval before conversion. Accepted revisions are locked; conversion, resource movements, invoices and cash allocations are guarded against repeated actions. Projects retain their accepted quotation, customer/site/manager, task dependencies and operational gates, linked jobs, budget/actual costs, invoice and activity. The two projects do not exist until their leads are converted.

The supplied `Quotation_261003_185747.pdf` is the PDF layout authority. Source header/footer artwork is embedded with provenance; dynamic text, item table, commercial terms, discount allocation, line VAT, totals and unsigned signature space are generated by jsPDF/AutoTable. Saved revisions download their own content and revision filenames. Quantities use up to three decimals and monetary rounding uses integer cents with largest-remainder discount allocation.

Storage migration preserves the previous version-1 saved records. Independent tab writes refresh the latest state; stale edits of an already-saved quotation revision are rejected. Restart removes only the selected lead's owned records and restores its stock movements; full reset restores the original demo and five fresh leads. Other demos' storage is untouched.

## Presenter route

Open `#leads`, choose a fresh lead and save an edited quotation. Preview/download its PDF, mark sent, record approval and convert. For a project, complete planning, reserve each resource, complete allocation, fulfil each linked job, complete execution/handover tasks and issue its invoice. Record a partial then final sample payment; customer history and project costs reconcile. Use the individual restart to repeat that lead independently. The legacy operational modules demonstrate partial sales deliveries and rental extensions; the five curated lead jobs use their explicit scoped transitions.

Phone verification uses Edge emulation; physical iOS Safari/Android devices were unavailable. All demo records stay on the browser/device; this release provides no cross-device synchronization or real sends, payments, installations or integrations.
