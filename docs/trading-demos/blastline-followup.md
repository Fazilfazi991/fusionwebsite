# Five-lead and project extension

The Blastline demo adds five independent, initially unconverted opportunities: two site projects, equipment sales, a dated physical rental and a customer-owned repair. The existing sales, rental, workshop and customer-ledger routes remain available. Data and external decisions/payments are fictional and browser-local.

Each lead requires an editable saved quotation, simulated sending and approval before conversion. Accepted revisions are locked; conversion, resource movements, invoices and cash allocations are guarded against repeated actions. Projects retain their accepted quotation, customer/site/manager, task dependencies and operational gates, linked jobs, budget/actual costs, invoice and activity. The two projects do not exist until their leads are converted.

The supplied `Quotation_261003_185747.pdf` is the PDF layout authority. Source header/footer artwork is embedded with provenance; dynamic text, item table, commercial terms, discount allocation, line VAT, totals and unsigned signature space are generated by jsPDF/AutoTable. Saved revisions download their own content and revision filenames. Quantities use up to three decimals and monetary rounding uses integer cents with largest-remainder discount allocation.

Storage migration preserves the previous version-1 saved records. Independent tab writes refresh the latest state; stale edits of an already-saved quotation revision are rejected. Restart removes only the selected lead's owned records and restores its stock movements; full reset restores the original demo and five fresh leads. Other demos' storage is untouched.

## Projects workspace patterns

Projects preserve the incumbent Blastline visual system in `public/blastline-crm/DESIGN.md` and the existing surface contract. The register counts converted Project records and separately lists Project opportunities awaiting conversion. A converted Sales, Rental or Repair lead creates jobs in its own module; it does not add a Project row.

The project summary pairs task-derived percentage and completed/total task counts with cost budget, actual costs and budget remaining. The numbered milestone sequence identifies the current milestone in words. Task rows expose assignee, deadline, named dependencies and a Ready, Blocked or Completed state; counted filters use pressed-button semantics. Blocked work explains its unmet gate. Completed work retains its saved note behind a native disclosure with a minimum 44px summary target and visible keyboard focus.

Project register, milestones, task rows, commercial content and opportunity activity share a 20px horizontal panel inset. Desktop pairs plan and budget; narrower layouts stack them. At 800px and below, the register and cost ledger use readable record layouts; at 480px and below, milestones become a vertical sequence and actions stack. AED amounts use tabular numerals and retain their full value.

Issue project invoice remains actionable before readiness so an attempt focuses inline feedback listing unfinished jobs and non-billing tasks by name. Open job, View tasks and Complete task actions lead directly to the remaining work. These affordances preserve the model's fulfilment and task gates; commercial closeout follows invoice issuance. Once ready, issuance creates one immutable invoice using the accepted quotation revision and VAT total. Sample partial/final receipts reconcile separately through the linked lead and customer history.

## Overview patterns

The Overview extends the same Blastline palette, Inter typography, outlined controls and calm bordered surfaces. Invoice allocation and operational mix pies pair their segments with labelled exact values; the former splits issued invoice value into allocated cash received and outstanding, while the latter counts actual Sales, Rental, Customer repair and Site service records once, including active and completed work and excluding cancelled rentals and declined repairs. Legend links reach the working finance and operational modules.

Six actionable lead-stage nodes show each walkthrough lead's current position once: New, Quoted, Approved, Fulfilment, Invoiced or Paid. Quoted includes draft, sent and declined revisions. Each node opens a populated or empty stage dialog with working lead navigation; arrows explain the sequence rather than a conversion rate. The cumulative invoice/receipt trend offers All saved dates, Last 7 days and Last 30 days, with shorter windows ending on the latest saved financial date and retaining earlier amounts in cumulative totals. A native disclosure exposes exact values by date with a minimum 44px summary target and visible focus.

Converted Projects receive task progress and paired budget/actual cost bars with exact AED values and Open project actions. L1 and L2 appear only after conversion; before that, the empty state links to their leads. Charts use a 20px content inset, stack on narrow screens and retain visible labels and amounts. Invoice, receipt, operational, lead and project figures follow the selected branch; branches without records display honest empty states. Useful-action queues remain available. Team follow-ups and customer activity preserve their existing global scope and identify it in their labels. This view derives from existing saved records without changing the model or storage.

## Presenter route

Start at `#overview` in Dubai. Explain cash received versus outstanding in the invoice pie, then the actual operational record mix. Open a lead-stage node and use Open lead to choose L1 or L2; a fresh walkthrough starts in New. Return to the Overview after conversion to show the Project's task progress, budget and actual cost bars; return again after fulfilment and sample receipts to show the saved financial trend and updated stage. Try the date filters and exact-value disclosure to reconcile the chart with its records.

From the selected lead, save an edited quotation, preview/download its PDF, mark sent, record approval and convert. For a project, complete planning, reserve each resource, complete allocation, fulfil each linked job, complete execution/handover tasks and issue its invoice. Record a partial then final sample payment; customer history and project costs reconcile. Use the individual restart to repeat that lead independently. The legacy operational modules demonstrate partial sales deliveries and rental extensions; the five curated lead jobs use their explicit scoped transitions.

Phone verification uses Edge emulation; physical iOS Safari/Android devices were unavailable. All demo records stay on the browser/device; this release provides no cross-device synchronization or real sends, payments, installations or integrations.
