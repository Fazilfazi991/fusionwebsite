# AquaFleet operations workspace

AquaFleet belongs to a small UAE tanker operator: the person assigning today's water deliveries, checking a prepaid filling-station balance and chasing credit collections. Dispatch is the focal task. A vehicle, driver, quantity in litres and source account must remain visible together.

Domain: tanker capacity, filling station, prepaid recharge, load debit, delivery site, cash collection, customer credit, fuel per trip and vehicle contribution. The color world draws on clear water blue, deeper pipe blue, white tanks, pale concrete, steel boundaries and muted route ink. The signature is the six-stage trip strip backed by an actual source-account debit and a connected vehicle cost breakdown. This appears in dispatch, trip details, water-source ledgers, fleet analytics and collections. Sales pipelines, oversized stat displays and decorative gradients are excluded.

Tokens are scoped in `styles.css`: water `#087e98`, deep water `#09657a`, water wash `#e9f5f7`, tank ink `#182f3c`, muted ink `#576d7a`, dispatch canvas `#f5f8fa`, white panels and pipe lines `#e0e8ed`. Semantic green, amber and red accompany written status labels. Existing locally hosted Inter supplies typography; AED and litre figures use tabular numerals.

The desktop navigation is 224px (200px below 1200px), with a related pale surface and a quiet divider. Content uses 32px outer spacing, 18px panel gaps, compact 13px body text, 10–11px table/meta labels, 14px panel titles, 24px figures and 26px page headings. Cards use 12px radii. Controls and primary actions target 44px. Depth comes from borders; native dialogs use an offset shadow. No animation is added. Reduced-motion styling prevents smooth scrolling.

Below 900px, the top-bar Menu opens native modal navigation with all ten modules; it does not hide modules behind inaccessible desktop controls. Cards become two columns, then single-column content panels below 600px. Tables scroll inside labeled, keyboard-focusable regions. Forms stack below 600px. Native dialogs trap focus, support Escape and restore focus to a working trigger after rerenders. Explicit focus rings, written statuses, semantic headers, labels and live feedback support keyboard and screen-reader use. The skip link becomes visible on focus.

The portfolio image is a WebP capture of the real seeded dashboard, generated by `scripts/water-transport-crm/preview.cjs`, using the existing portfolio asset format. It shows the actual interface rather than an independently invented visual. Source, report and mobile acceptance evidence comes from the browser script; no external images, fonts, chart libraries or runtime dependencies are needed by this demo.
